About the studio

Where tribal tradition
meets modern ink.

Tribal Tattoo Garden & Piercing Studio is built around one idea: every piece should mean something. Working out of Mfundopark Whitehouse in Vosloorus, our artists blend bold tribal-inspired line work with contemporary technique — from delicate first tattoos to full sleeves that take shape over several sessions.

Every station is set up with single-use needles, medical-grade sterilisation and a genuine focus on comfort, so first-timers and collectors alike leave with work they're proud to show off.

Meet the artist

Ntates

Owner & Tattoo Artist

Ntates founded Tribal Tattoo Garden & Piercing Studio to give Vosloorus a space where tattooing is treated as a craft, not a quick transaction. Every design starts as a real conversation — what it means, where it sits on the body, how it'll age — long before the machine touches skin.

The studio's tribal-inspired style runs through original linework, black & grey portraits and colour pieces alike, always built around clean lines that hold up years down the line.
